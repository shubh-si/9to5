Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Last updated: September 2025

1. Introduction & Overview

At JobPortal ("JobPortal", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting the confidential information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, process, and safeguard your personal details, resume attachments, application logs, and company profile records.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to the platform when you create an account, build a seeker profile, post a job opening, or submit a job application:

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, contact phone number, and password hashes.
  • Job Seeker Profiles & Resumes: Professional headline, bio, location, target salary, employment history, education, uploaded PDF/Word resume documents, and links to GitHub/LinkedIn/portfolio websites.
  • Employer Profiles: Company name, logo, industry specialization, company size, website URL, and business descriptions.
  • Application Data: Job application submissions, cover letters, and application review status changes.
3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To enable job seekers to apply directly to verified employers.
  • To allow hiring managers to evaluate applications, download resumes, and manage hiring pipelines.
  • To provide real-time status notifications and email verification tokens.
  • To prevent fraud, eliminate duplicate applications, and uphold platform quality standards.
4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers. Your resume and application details are only accessible to the specific hiring organizations to which you explicitly apply, or to verified employers through our candidate sourcing tool if you choose to make your seeker profile visible.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, you maintain full ownership of your data. You may update your resume, modify your profile, or request complete account deletion at any time by contacting our privacy officer at privacy@jobportal.pro.
